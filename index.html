Before you get in touch

A few things you may want to know.

If your question is not covered here, call us. We would be happy to understand your requirement.

Which commodities do you deal in?

We handle brokerage enquiries for cattle feed, edible oil, edible oil seeds and Kapasiya Khod, also known as Kapasiya Khal, Binola Khal or cottonseed cake.

Do you work with buyers as well as sellers?

Yes. We welcome relevant enquiries from buyers, sellers, farmers, manufacturers, traders and cattle-feed businesses.

Which locations do you serve?

Ravi Brokers is based in Rajkot and welcomes commodity brokerage enquiries from across Gujarat.

What details should I share?

Please share whether you want to buy or sell, the commodity, approximate quantity, grade or quality if known, location and expected timing. We can discuss the rest directly.

Can you confirm today's rate or availability?

Rates and availability can change based on commodity, grade, quantity, location and timing. We discuss and confirm them directly for each enquiry. No rate is guaranteed on this website.

Does sending this form place an order?

No. The form only prepares a WhatsApp message. An order, price, availability and transaction terms are confirmed only after direct discussion and agreement.
